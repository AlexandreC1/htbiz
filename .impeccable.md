## Design Context

### Users
Haitian business owners managing listings, reviews, and listing performance.

### Brand Personality
Clear, professional, approachable. The owner should feel confident about what
was saved and what needs attention.

### Aesthetic Direction
The user confirmed a clean, professional dashboard with icons and labels.
Preserve the app's existing blue identity and translated French, Creole, and
English labels. Prefer readable mobile layouts over dense statistic rows.

### Design Principles
- Pair recognizable icons with explicit labels; replace decorative emoji.
- Keep form content on failure and confirm success only after persistence.
- Distinguish unavailable data from genuine zero activity.
- Make primary tasks easy to reach on small screens and with large text.
- Explain analytics collection and restrict detailed access to administrators.
